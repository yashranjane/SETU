⚓ How This Works: GFR 2017 Rule 173 (Govt Technology Trial Framework)
Normally in government tenders, officers are forced to pick the lowest bidder (L1), even if their product fails. Under GFR 2017 Rule 173, government departments have the legal power to bypass tenders and run safe, controlled field trials (Sandboxes) to test if innovative technology actually works before spending full public funds:
  • Smart 4-Factor AI Matching: Govt officers state their civic problem; AI instantly matches verified Indian startups on technical capability.
  • Safe 3-Stage Trial Agreement (MoU): Budget is locked safely and paid step-by-step: 20% Setup, 50% Working Proof, and 30% Final Handover.
  • 100% CVC Protection: Every approval and payment is locked in an unbreakable SHA-256 audit ledger.
  • GeM Fast-Track Certificate: Once the trial passes, the seller gets an official certificate to sell on GeM without tenders!
