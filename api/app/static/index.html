⚖ How This Works: GFR 2017 Rule 173 (Govt Technology Trial Framework)
Normally in government tenders, officers are forced to pick the lowest bidder (L1), even if their product fails. Under GFR 2017 Rule 173, government departments have the legal power to bypass tenders and run safe, controlled field trials (Sandboxes) to test if innovative technology actually works before spending full public funds:
  • Smart 4-Factor AI Matching: Govt officers state their civic problem (e.g. drain flood sensors or drone surveillance); AI instantly matches verified Indian startups and GeM sellers on technical capability.
  • Safe 3-Stage Trial Agreement (MoU): No advance loss! Budget is locked safely and paid step-by-step: 20% Setup, 50% Working Proof (must work in the field), and 30% Final Handover.
  • 100% Vigilance & CVC Protection: Every trial approval, sensor data proof, and payment release is locked in an unbreakable digital audit ledger with SHA-256 proof.
  • GeM Fast-Track Purchase Certificate: Once the product successfully passes the field trial, the seller gets an official certificate to sell directly to any government department in India on GeM without tenders!
