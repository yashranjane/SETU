4. Data Localization & Cybersecurity Governance

In accordance with the National Data Governance Framework and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act:

  1. Sovereign Data Residency: All government data, sensor telemetry, GIS coordinates, and user records generated during the pilot must reside strictly on Indian data center infrastructure. Zero data shall be egressed overseas.
  2. Information Security: The Startup shall maintain AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all APIs and communication channels.
  3. Auditability: All configuration modifications, state transitions, and milestone submissions shall be logged immutably via SETU cryptographic audit chains.
5. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
  1. Background IP: The Startup shall retain full, exclusive ownership of all pre-existing Intellectual Property, proprietary algorithms, and models.
  2. Government License: The Procuring Entity receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use data and analytics dashboards generated during the pilot for municipal operations.
  3. Scale-Up Option: Upon successful completion of M3 with ScaleUpRecommended status, the Procuring Entity retains the right to procure enterprise rollout under GFR 173 without repeat tender.
6. Default, Termination & Dispute Resolution

Either party may terminate this MoU with 14 days' written notice in the event of material non-performance. Any disputes arising hereunder shall be referred to the Secretary (IT), Government of Maharashtra, whose decision shall be final and binding.
